COMPUTATIONAL PHYSICS · MATERIALS SCIENCE

Meet Yijing Zuo

I am a PhD student at Wuhan University’s National Elite Institute of Engineering. I received my B.Sc. from the School of Physics and Technology at Wuhan University in 2025.

My research connects phonon physics, atomistic simulation, and machine learning to understand and predict the properties of materials. I am particularly interested in anomalous thermal transport under compression, anharmonic lattice dynamics, and AI-assisted materials discovery.

RESEARCH UPDATE

Our study of compression-induced anomalous thermal transport in NaB(CN)4 is published in Physical Review B 114, 154307 (2026).

Research & experience

03
THERMAL TRANSPORT

Understanding heat flow under compression

Combining first-principles calculations, high-order force constants, phonon renormalization and three- and four-phonon scattering to investigate anomalous thermal transport. Machine-learned potentials and nonequilibrium molecular dynamics provide complementary atomistic insight.

AI FOR SCIENCE

Learning molecular and materials properties

Graph neural networks and interpretable learning for property prediction and materials screening. Recent project work includes adapting a pretrained MolCLR / GIN model for multitask molecular-property prediction and high-energy fuel screening.
